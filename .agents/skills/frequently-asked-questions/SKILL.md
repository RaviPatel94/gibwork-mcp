# Gibwork FAQ

Reference docs: https://docs.gib.work/

Answer user questions using the Q&A entries below. Keep answers short and in numbered-step format. If a question isn't covered here, say so and point the user to https://docs.gib.work/ rather than guessing.

## Q: How do I back up my private key?
1. Open your wallet
2. Click the three dots (⋮)
3. Click **Export Private Key**
4. Copy the key and save it somewhere secure (never share it or store it in plain text online)

## Q: How do I create a profile?
1. Click your profile image on the bottom navbar
2. Select a category of your expertise
3. Set the minimum amount anyone must pay before messaging you
4. Save

## Q: Where do I find all my paid requests?
Click the mail icon in the top-right corner.

## Q: How do I check task details?
1. Click a task
2. Click the reward pool amount

## Q: How do I check referrals?
1. Click **Wallet**
2. Click **Refer and Earn**

## Q: How can I maximize my earnings as a beginner?
- Connect all accounts and set up an ATA-enabled wallet — an unconnected wallet costs higher platform fees
- Share your referral link — you get paid when someone you refer earns or creates a task
- Focus on tasks that match your actual skills instead of low-effort submissions on everything
- Join the Gibwork Discord — community paid events (trivia, games, engagement posts) are easy extra income
- Consider a Plus subscription — many Plus users earn back several times the fee; the yearly plan is cheaper than monthly

## Q: What happens after a paid request is accepted?
- The request becomes the shared workspace for the client and freelancer
- Both sides can clarify the brief, ask questions, request changes, and share the final deliverable
- The locked request amount can't be changed if scope grows — the freelancer must ask the client to send the extra USDC as a separate payment

## Q: How are funds protected on a paid request?
- If the freelancer doesn't respond within a 2-day window, the client can recover the funds
- If the deadline passes with no delivery, the client can get a refund
- Once work is delivered and approved, payment is released and final on-chain

## Q: Should I set up a skill/category, or send a paid request?
- To offer a skill: set your category and request price
- To get work done: use Explore, check a freelancer's earnings and completed bounties, then send a Paid Request instead of a cold DM
